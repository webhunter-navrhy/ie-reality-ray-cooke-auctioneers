Description

RAY COOKE AUCTIONEERS are delighted to present this fantastic heavily extended 3-bedroom semi-detached family home located on a peaceful cul de sac to the market in the ever-popular Heatherview Lawn, Aylesbury. Heatherview Lawn is ideally located at the foothills of the Dublin Mountains and is always in high demand as it is within easy reach of all local amenities including shopping facilities, a variety of bars & restaurants, public transport, an extensive choice of sports grounds & leisure facilities, a great mix of primary & secondary schools. The area is well serviced with excellent bus routes and road networks and is only a 20-minute walk to the Square shopping centre and red line Luas stop.

Bright, airy and most spacious internal living accommodation of 120 sqm briefly comprises of entrance hallway with guest bathroom, large living room with feature fireplace, large open plan fully fitted kitchen/dining area to the rear. On the first floor you have three generous bedrooms with built in wardrobes and main family bathroom. The attic has been converted here and is currently being used as a 4th bedroom but could be used for so many different purposes. To the rear of the property you have a private & enclosed garden that comes of the lowest maintenance with a private patio & pergola area making it perfect for relaxing on them long sunny summer evenings with the use of a shed which is great for additional storage.

No. 33 comes to the market in turnkey condition throughout having been lovingly maintained by its current owners with no expense spared when upgrading in recent years. Houses like this don't often come to the market and it has many additional features such as converted attic, top quality fixtures & fittings, high energy rating, gas fired central heating and plenty of additional storage to name but a few.

This one is prime for a wide range of buyers such as 1st time buyers looking to get on the property ladder & any growing family looking to settle roots in a highly sought-after area.

Viewing is highly advised, please contact Ray Cooke Auctioneers for further information or to arrange viewing!
